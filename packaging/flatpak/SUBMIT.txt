Description:
Clocki is a tiny always-on-top focus timer and clock overlay. It counts the hours you focus each day and
resets at local midnight. Electron app, X11 (XWayland) because staying on top is not possible for native
Wayland clients.

AI disclosure:
Most of the application code and the Flatpak packaging were written with AI coding assistants
(Claude, Codex) under my direction; I reviewed, tested and maintain it.
